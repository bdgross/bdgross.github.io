About me

  • 15-plus years at The Washington Post. Like many news operations, The Post has navigated significant change. I was asked to step into the department head role to guide the design team through a restructuring. We made real progress on print automation and story format optimization. Ultimately the role was eliminated as part of a larger reorganization.
