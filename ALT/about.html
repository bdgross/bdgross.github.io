Why work with me

I help organizations evolve through digital transformation and content strategy. My process involves listening carefully, setting clear expectations, making thoughtful decisions and following through.


  • I provide creative direction through a shared vision for consistent brand identity, quality and innovation.
  • I align processes for smarter workflows and more effective operations, and I identify reusable tools give creative teams more time to do great work.
  • I develop talent and build high-performing teams through mentoring and organizational structures that help people grow and perform.
  • I position teams at the forefront of AI exploration through internal research by advising on initiatives for workflow efficiency and integration.

Background

Like many news operations, The Washington Post has navigated significant change. After 10 years as deputy design director, I was asked to step into the department head role to guide the 50-member design team through a restructuring. We made real progress on print automation and story format optimization. Ultimately the role was eliminated as part of a larger reorganization in early 2026.
