I turn innovative visual storytelling and ambitious graphic design into a competitive advantage through strategic leadership.



My approach

Put readers first as part of an audience-first vision. Create the conditions for risk-taking by building high-performing teams.


Organizational value

Premium experiences for paying subscribers. Talent development for sustainable excellence and the agility to evolve as business priorities change.


Practical application

Case studies for deeply engaging and immersive presentations, built through collaboration and guided by editorial judgment. It starts by understanding the story, defining the purpose, identifing a visual hook and choosing the format that best serves the narrative. Prioritize clarity, accuracy, engagement and trust.

Recognition and impact

Award-winning journalism, powered by exceptional visual storytelling: As a director-level leader, recruited and developed top creative talent, expanded the size of the department and made design integral from the earliest stages. Provided direction, editorial oversight and support that enabled the team to contribute on 14 Pulitzer Prize-winning projects and 17 Pulitzer finalists over the past decade.

The Washington Post staff awards:

  • The Post earned two Pulitzer Prizes in 2026 with contributions from designers on Social Security wait times and He’s dying. She’s pregnant.
  • The Society for News Design also recognized The Post with Best in Show in 2026 for coverage of the Middle East conflict along with two other top individual honors. The Post previously won Best in Show in 2020 and 2024.

SND Secretary Rebecca Pazos
