Scaling and Sustaining Success

I solve organizational challenges by elevating visual storytelling and appling systems thinking to people, processes and priorities, guided by an editorial perspective.


helping organizations navigate digital transformation, reorganizations and shifting priorities

Outcomes

Sustainable success starts with aligning people, processes and priorities. My approach involves listening carefully, setting clear expectations, making thoughtful decisions and following through.

  • A clear creative direction: Including a stronger brand identity and a shared vision for quality, consistency and innovation.
  • A more effective operation: Better workflows, smarter processes and tools that give creative teams more time to do great work.
  • A stronger team: Talent development, mentoring and organizational structures that help people grow and perform.

Experience

Developed talent and led a large department within the fast-paced newsroom of The Washington Post.

Digital transformation: Transitioned traditional print designers into news developers through targeted training and skill sharing, while doubling the staff from 25 to 50 and adding managers to meet demand for ambition and quality. Navigated multiple reorganizations and introduced dedicated roles for project editing, illustrated reporting, motion graphics and digital infrastructure.

Reusable tools: Formed a task force to launch repeatable mobile-first visual templates, resulting in eight new formats (galleries, quizzes, polls) rapidly adopted by the newsroom on more than a hundred stories in the first year.

Documentation: Drove a visuals style guide and color palette update, unblocking project delays and aligning stakeholders.

AI adoption: Established an advisory group and led cross-department summits to accelerate workflow efficiency at The Post.

Recent consulting and freelance clients include:

  • Wake Forest Magazine (digital modernization and training)
  • The Philadelphia Inquirer (special section and art direction)
  • The University of Tampa Journal (conceptual magazine design)

Cross-functional strength and strategic flexibility

Background

15-plus years at The Washington Post including roles as Department Head and Deputy Design Director. Previously worked at the Boston Globe and San Diego Union-Tribune.

  • Instructor for graduate-level Digital Design and Development course with the Philip Merrill School of Journalism at the University of Maryland during the fall 2026 semester.
  • Organized and ran Society for News Design digital competition from 2019-2024, judged in 2005 and 2016, and served as team captain for judging teams in 2025 and 2026. Five-time SND workshop speaker.
  • Poynter Leadership Academy (2006, 2015).
  • Bachelor's degree in journalism from Southern Illinois University Carbondale.
