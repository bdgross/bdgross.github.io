The Attack

Challenge and objective: To engage and guide readers through a deeply reported three-part multimedia project. As design editor, I contributed early concepts for a cinematic aesthetic, along with framing, structure, and navigation progress for each chapter. Result: I led a collaborative effort to deliver a boundary-pushing build with footnotes, SMS embeds and audio for the defining record of Jan. 6. Pulitzer Prize for Public Service.

American Icon

Challenge: Launch seven interactive stories on the same day across platforms as a cohesive investigative package in collaboration with photo and graphics. Objectives: Signal the importance of the findings while carefully handling sensitive content for both readers and the staff. Result: A direct and transparent approach brought new focus to the topic from readers and policy makers. Pulitzer Prize for National Reporting, Best in Show from the Society for News Design and gold medal from the Society of Publication Designers.

Post Next 50

Launched a branding project to profile the people expected to shape society in the year ahead with an inaugural list and follow-up. Challenge: Reach new readers via a sub brand with strong social presence and mobile emphasis. Objectives: Distinctive type and smart navigation with clear UX while staying part of larger brand. Result: Balanced multi-platform workflow, coordinated live event and introduced media kits.

Klamath River

Turned successful experiments such as this mobile-first visual narrative into repeatable practices without making the work formulaic. Challenge: Combine best of tap and scroll UX to develop a new tool informed by user testing. Objectives: Seamless transition between autoplay video, text and visuals optimized for mobile UX. Result: Reporters became active partners in integrating text with visuals for immersive experience. SND Award of Excellence.

7-Minute Workout

Illustrated videos served as a how-to guide for this popular workout. Challenge: Multi-part series and reader service across a variety of formats and access points. Objectives: Engaging, variable and approachable art style for demonstrating workout steps. Result: Tracking data led to a button modification that tripled the click rate to 24 percent. SND silver and three bronze medals, SPD medal finalist. The Washington Post, Jan. 9, 2025

Simone Biles

Challenge: Demonstrate moves of high-profile athlete in a fresh visual style, tailored for mobile. Objectives: Collaboration with animation studio for accuracy and tight timeline for Olympics preview. Result: Adapted for multiple downstream platforms, reaching deeply engaged social audience. Seven awards from Society for News Design, including three silver medals and a bronze. Instagram version with more than 750,000 likes. The Washington Post, July 28, 2024

Mueller Report Illustrated

Guided design from concept to publication for this six-chapter digital investigation. Challenge: Bring a government report to life with “graphic novel” scenes as part of small secluded team. Objectives: Incorporate audio and analysis within complex UX, ensure accuracy of illustrations. Result: Became a model for getting design an early seat at the table and integratings visuals with text. Created cover design for the 208-page Scribner book edition. Best in Show from the Society for News Design. The Washington Post, November 2019
